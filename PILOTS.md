# Premade pilot texts (drafts, Nov 5 2026)

Same shape as Chad Beefsteel's in the GDD. Numbers (credits, reputation, systems) are placeholders.

## Reginald Fanshawe-Buttersworth III (easy)
Hauler, ~25,000 credits, AURORA, Federation +25, Rebels -20.
Narration: Welcome to the Frontier. Out here, fortunes are made, empires collapse, and entire civilizations hang in the balance. Some pilots seek glory. Others seek wealth, freedom, or adventure. Reginald Fanshawe-Buttersworth III is looking for someone to carry his bags. His ship is enormous, unarmed, and has a fully stocked tea cabinet. His fortune is considerable, for the moment. His family's lawyers have been instructed to stop returning his calls. And somewhere on the trade lanes, a bored pirate has spotted a very large, very slow ship with excellent catering. Good luck, Reginald. You're going to need it.
Summary: Reginald Fanshawe-Buttersworth III was born into a fortune, educated at four universities he was asked to leave, and cut off after losing the family flagship in a wager he still insists he was winning. He has one cargo ship, a trust fund, and the unshakeable belief that every problem can be solved by hiring someone else.
Traits: piloting poor, combat nonexistent, luck average, ego towering, finances "excellent until it isn't".
Recognition: an admiral's grave speech about the fleet's finances ends "And if Buttersworth is involved, check the catering budget."
NPC line: "Is that the Buttersworth who tried to pay a customs fine with a signed napkin?"

## Zsa-Zsa Blastwell (combat)
Raider, ~3,000 credits plus a 1,500 debt due in 30 days to a Syndicate lender, CINDER, Federation -30, Rebels +10.
Narration: ...Zsa-Zsa Blastwell is looking for the next thing to overtake. Her ship is fast, loud, and has been banned from nine racing circuits. Her licence is real, which surprised everyone including her. Her lender is patient, and has stopped being patient. And somewhere out here, a referee is still pressing charges. Good luck, Zsa-Zsa. You're going to need it.
Summary: Zsa-Zsa Blastwell won forty-one races, was disqualified from thirty of them, and parked a freighter inside a station she was only supposed to be passing. She has one very fast ship, one very patient creditor, and a driving philosophy that braking is a form of surrender.
Traits: piloting superb, combat excellent, luck dubious, ego large, finances "in arrears".
Recognition: a solemn speech about the starlanes ends "And if Blastwell is flying, clear the lanes."
NPC line: "Blastwell? The one who parked a freighter inside a station?"

## Professor Marigold Quasar-Pennywhistle (explorer)
Wayfarer with a big fuel tank, ~4,000 credits, one free star chart, KESTREL, Federation -15, Rebels +30.
Narration: ...Professor Marigold Quasar-Pennywhistle is looking for a comet. Her ship has an extra fuel tank, a very good telescope, and no guns worth mentioning. Her funding has been cancelled twice and quietly restored once. Her colleagues say the comet doesn't exist. Her cat disagrees. Good luck, Marigold. You're going to need it.
Summary: Professor Marigold Quasar-Pennywhistle holds three doctorates, one retracted paper, and a firm belief that the universe is hiding something just past the next jump. She has one stubbornly overfuelled ship, one cat, and no sense of danger whatsoever.
Traits: piloting average, combat poor, luck surprisingly good, ego "scientific", finances "grants".
Recognition: a stern speech about unexplored space ends "And if Pennywhistle has found something, please let it be a comet."
NPC line: "You're the Pennywhistle who found the cat comet! Is it real?" ("It's real.")

## Custom pilot
Pick a name, a starting ship, then a difficulty (sets reputation and start system):
Easy: AURORA-type Federation core, Fed +20, Rebels -10, ~5,000 credits.
Medium: Independent hub, 0/0, ~3,000 credits.
Hard: Syndicate rim, Fed -30, Rebels -30, ~1,000 credits.
Narration: Welcome to the Frontier. ...Some pilots seek glory. Others seek wealth, freedom, or adventure. [NAME] is looking for an explanation. The ship's logs begin nineteen days ago. The cargo hold contains a single sealed crate that hums. The registration lists an owner who does not appear to exist, and the previous pilot's seat is still warm. Nobody has asked who you are, which is either very good news or very bad news. Good luck, [NAME]. You're going to need it, and nobody is going to say so out loud.
Summary: [NAME] was born on a date that doesn't appear on any record, trained at an institution that no longer exists, and has been employed by an organisation that has since denied hiring anyone. Someone somewhere certainly knows what [NAME] is for. It isn't [NAME], and it isn't anyone nearby.
Traits: all shown as unknown. NPCs get the name wrong ("Nate? Nolan?") until the reputation grows.
